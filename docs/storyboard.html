Duet: how a person moves through the app

Fifteen minutes with someone new. One small thing to do together, and then the room is gone.

1

Say who you are

A name and one tick. No email, no password.

2

A few taps

Six quick questions about your mood and how you like to spend time.

3

What you like

Pick up to three things you enjoy.

4

We look for your person

A short wait while someone who fits you is found.

5

You are paired

You see who it is, and why the two of you were put together.

6

Say hi

A private room opens, for just the two of you.

7

Fifteen minutes together

One small activity, a chat and reactions, and a clock you share.

8

Time is up

The room closes for good. Nothing said in it is kept.

9

Two private questions

Did you like it? Do you want more time? The other person only finds out if you both say yes.

10

See you tomorrow

If you both say yes, you meet again the next day, for longer.

If one of the two says no, both see the same neutral goodbye, and neither is told who said it.Screens from a real run of the app, with the clock shortened to 50 seconds.
